Product Requirements Document (PRD): Ground Truth 3D Homepage

1\. Overview

**Project Name:** Ground Truth 3D Homepage Content Update

**Role:** Act as a an expert UX/UI designer and conversion copywriter with a clean, spare architect-like design sense

**Design Aesthetic:** Commercially oriented, architectural aesthetic. Mostly Black, white and gray tones with shots of color to feature critical information

**Voice:** Authoritative but friendly

**Goal:** To establish the Ground Truth 3D as the authoritative source for existing conditions documentation services, clearly articulating the company's value proposition to target professional audiences.

**Target Audience:**  
The homepage content is focused on the unique needs of Owners, Architects, and Builders. The audience also includes interior architects and contractors.

**Technology/Expertise:**  
The company provides LiDAR based commercial and residential existing conditions documentation, leveraging 10 years of experience documenting 100s of buildings.
