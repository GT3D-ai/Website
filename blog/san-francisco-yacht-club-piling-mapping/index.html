Case study

San Francisco Yacht Club — piling mapping

Under-pier reality capture where tides, access, and geometry all matter

February 18, 2026

Marine documentation is a different problem from a typical interior scan. You work around water, limited windows for access, and safety rules that dictate how and when crews can be under a structure. The goal is the same as on land: a trustworthy 3D record—but the logistics are rarely generic.

For piling and under-deck work, teams need clear deliverables: registered point clouds, measurable linework, and context that engineers can use for assessment and maintenance planning. The scan isn’t “for its own sake”—it’s input to decisions about load paths, deterioration, and future repairs.

What we optimize for

  • Access and sequencing — capturing coverage without putting people in unnecessary exposure.
  • Registration quality — tying scans together so distances and alignments hold when the model moves into CAD or BIM.
  • Deliverable fit — matching level of detail to how the owner and engineer will use the data.
