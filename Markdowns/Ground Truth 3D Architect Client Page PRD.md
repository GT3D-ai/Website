* **Content:**  
  Ground Truth is transforming project initiation by delivering comprehensive residential and commercial existing conditions plans and models using cutting-edge technology. We couple **LiDAR scanning** with high-resolution **360° photography** to create a technological fusion that eliminates the uncertainty of traditional methods. This process provides accurate data that serves as an unimpeachable foundation for all subsequent design work. By providing verifiable accuracy and remarkable speed, we allow architects to focus their expertise on design innovation, rather than spending time chasing field dimensions.**Documentation Options for Every Project Scope**  
    
  Ground Truth’s offerings are structured to integrate seamlessly into existing design workflows, providing deliverables in industry-standard formats like Revit, AutoCAD, and Sketchup. Our core services span the spectrum of documentation needs:  
* **2D Documentation:** Solutions range from rapid, precise 2D floor plans, sections, and reflected ceiling plans for partial buildings (tenant improvements, interior remodels) to a complete, accurate set of base drawings for a full structure. We also offer **2D Site Documentation**, critical for urban projects and zoning compliance, capturing topography, utilities, and major site elements.  
* **3D Building Documentation (BIM):** This service provides the foundation for modern Building Information Modeling (BIM) by delivering a rich 3D Revit 2026 model, complete with accurate geometry and spatial relationships of the existing structure. We also offer comprehensive **3D Building and Site Documentation** for holistic BIM environments.  
* **Specialized Deliverables:** Enhanced services include Detailed Interior Elevations (essential for cabinet design and complex detailing), Electrical Switch and Fixture Mapping (vital for MEP coordination), and Enhanced Structural Detailing (focused documentation of critical elements like beam sizes and column locations). We can also create **Schematic 3D Fly-throughs** to aid in early client presentations.  
  **A Streamlined, Reliable Process**  
    
  The efficiency of Ground Truth’s service is based on an optimized, five-step workflow that ensures rapid data acquisition and conversion into usable architectural assets:  
1. **Scope and Strategy Definition:** We consult with your team to establish specific project goals, preferred file formats (e.g., AutoCAD layers), and Level of Development (LOD) for 3D models.  
2. **On-Site LiDAR Data Acquisition:** Reality capture technicians are deployed rapidly, using state-of-the-art LiDAR scanners to generate a dense point cloud and 360° photography. This non-invasive process minimizes disruption and is fast, often capturing residential structures in just 4-5 hours.  
3. **Data Processing and Registration:** Raw data is meticulously registered, stitching all individual scans and photos into a unified, measurable point cloud—the single source of truth for all subsequent deliverables.  
4. **Deliverable Generation and Modeling:** Experienced drafters and BIM specialists convert the verified point cloud into clean, logical 2D drawings and 3D models built to the specified LOD.  
5. **Final Quality Assurance and Delivery:** A final check confirms dimensional accuracy. Assets are delivered digitally, often with a web viewer allowing architects to virtually “walk through” the 360° imagery from any web browser.  
   **Strategic Investment and ROI**  
     
   Utilizing LiDAR documentation dramatically accelerates project timelines, compressing weeks of traditional field work and drafting into days for scanning and a predictable turnaround for modeling. This efficiency allows schematic design to begin weeks sooner, leading to faster project completion and quicker billing cycles.  
     
   The cost of advanced documentation is competitive with manual methods, and the long-term ROI is exceptional. By eliminating dimensional errors, Ground Truth significantly mitigates the risk of costly change orders and delays during construction—a single late-stage error can easily outweigh the entire cost of the service. Furthermore, immediate access to an accurate 3D BIM model streamlines coordination with MEP and Structural engineering disciplines, reducing conflicts before they reach the job site.  
     
   Accurate existing conditions documentation is the strategic imperative for successful, risk-managed architectural practice. Contact Ground Truth today to discuss how our documentation can become your new competitive edge.
